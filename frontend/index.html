Quality & Blur Boundaries

Review Blur Threshold: < 60.0
Calculated via variance of the Laplacian kernel. Documents below 60.0 are routed to the Review Queue.
Failure Blur Threshold: < 30.0
Extremely unreadable/corrupted captures are marked FAILED immediately to avoid OCR hallucinations.
Brightness Bounds: 40 – 230
Mean pixel luminance checks. Under 40 (underexposed) or over 230 (blown out) trigger audit flags.
Contrast Minimum: 25.0
Standard deviation of grayscale intensities ensuring readable ink contrast.

Extraction & Ambiguity Parameters

Ambiguity Delta Threshold: 0.08
If top two candidates differ by ≤ 0.08 confidence, document is flagged for review.
Minimum Confidence: 0.70
Fields extracted with overall confidence < 0.70 fail validation and require approval.
Geometry Proximity Algorithm
Evaluates fixed-region, right-of-label, below-label, and nearest-centroid vectors deterministically.
